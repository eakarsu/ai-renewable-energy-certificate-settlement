# Renewable Energy Certificate Settlement

Reconcile renewable certificate creation, delivery, pricing, retirement, and cash settlement across registries and contracts.

**Primary buyer:** Renewable generators, utilities, and energy traders. **Evidence:** REC contracts, asset eligibility, meter production, registry issuances, vintages, retirements, market prices, transfers, invoices, and cash.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- REC contract library
- Asset eligibility registry
- Meter production ingestion
- Certificate issuance matching
- Vintage validation
- Product class mapping
- Delivery schedule control
- Market price calculation
- Transfer confirmation
- Retirement evidence
- Shortfall penalty calculation
- Invoice settlement matching
- Registry dispute workflow
- Cash reconciliation
- Asset market analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4720>. API: `5720`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
